E. Ablations
Table V isolates the controller. Absolute-only control
can point toward the demonstrated manifold but lacks the
local precision needed to finish tasks. Fixed fusion harms
nominal execution while still under-correcting disturbances.
Only adaptive fusion is strong in both regimes, supporting
a continuous switch between local execution and global
recovery.
Table VI isolates representation and training. Replacing
RVQ with an unconstrained MLP collapses the absolute
branch on LIBERO-10, so a discrete demonstrated-manifold
anchor is essential in this configuration. Symmetric aug-
mentation weakens both nominal and perturbed performance
by reducing the intended specialization between a sensitive
relative head and a robust absolute head. Removing adaptive
CFG likewise produces large drops, particularly off distribu-
tion.
a) Fusion on clean trajectories.: Across 2,000 clean

percentile is 0.339, and only 1.7% of steps exceed 0.5.
